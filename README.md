# Group 9 Scene Understanding

## Ten de tai

Phan tich ngu canh giao thong dua tren Semantic Segmentation va Depth Estimation.

## Mo ta ngan

De tai xu ly anh duong pho de hieu ngu canh giao thong. He thong nhan mot anh dau vao, sau do tao ra hai loai ket qua chinh:

- Semantic segmentation: phan vung tung pixel theo lop nhu road, car, sky, sidewalk, person.
- Depth map: uoc luong vung nao gan, vung nao xa camera.

Khi ket hop segmentation va depth, he thong co the hieu canh tot hon, vi khong chi biet trong anh co gi ma con biet cac vung do nam gan hay xa. Day la nen tang cho cac bai toan nhu ho tro lai xe, camera giao thong va scene understanding.
